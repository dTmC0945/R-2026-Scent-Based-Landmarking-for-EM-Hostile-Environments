Scent-Based Landmark and Communication
for EM Hostile Environments


Daniel Tunç McGuiness Department of Mechatronics, MCI, Innsbruck, Austria
Daniel.McGuiness@mci.edu

Abstract. An effective use of mobile robots is their ability to traverse environments which can be hostile or dangerous to human life. This problem is further accentuated with environments where communications using electromagnetic wave may prove ineffective, such as caves, or collapsed infrastructure. This paper proposes a method of establishing a simple communication between robots using chemicals. The communication relies on the pathfinder robot leaving scent packages to be picked up by another robot which can interpret the action. In addition a simple package structure is proposed along with preliminary information capacity of communication is analysed. The results show that if proper care is given on the selection of chemicals, temporary landmarks could be established for robots to communicate without effecting the surrounding environment.

Keywords: navigation, communication, mobile robotics, molecular communications
